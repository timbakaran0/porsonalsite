Intent vs.
Discovery

SEO

The Action Plan

Stop Choosing.
Use the 70/30 Split.

Service Business · Gym, Agency, Plumber
SEO First (70%) · Social for Proof (30%)
Nobody opens Instagram to find a plumber. Build your Google presence first. Use social only to show you're real and active.
Product Business · Clothing, Gadgets, Food
Social First (70%) · SEO for Brand (30%)
People buy clothes because they look great on video. Focus on reels + micro-influencers. SEO just ensures your brand name ranks.

Avoid These Traps

3 Mistakes That
Kill Small Businesses
Online

✕
The Ghost Town Website
Pouring everything into Instagram while your site takes 20 seconds to load. That bio link is costing you customers every day.
✕
Ignoring Search Intent
"History of Gyms" gets no leads. "Join a Gym in Rajkot" gets customers. Write for buyers, not historians.
✕
Being Everywhere at Once
Twitter + LinkedIn + Instagram + TikTok + Pinterest. Pick ONE platform. Master it before expanding.
