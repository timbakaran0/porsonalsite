Founder and trainer

Meet Anupama Mishra.

Anupama Mishra is the founder and driving force behind International Core Alignment Training in Wakad, PCMC, Pune.

Her saved ICAT profile describes a deep interest in spirituality, personal development and human behaviour. She holds a postgraduate degree in Yogic Science and a degree in Science, and is listed as an internationally certified Neuro-Linguistic Programming trainer, life transformation coach, meditation coach and former banker.

Through NLP training, life coaching and meditation classes, ICAT aims to help people live with greater authenticity, alignment, abundance and confidence.
